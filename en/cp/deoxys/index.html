Deoxys is weak to Bug, Ghost, Dark and resists Fighting, Psychic. Bug-type moves deal the most damage to it. In Windy weather its moves are boosted, and catches get the weather boost (higher level and CP).

Deoxys's stats lean towards very high Attack (Attack 345 / Defense 115 / Stamina 137), so it is best used as a damage dealer in raids.

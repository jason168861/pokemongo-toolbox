Volcarona is weak to Rock, Water, Flying (double weakness: Rock) and resists Grass, Ice, Fighting, Bug. Rock-type moves deal the most damage to it. In Rain, Sunny weather its moves are boosted, and catches get the weather boost (higher level and CP).

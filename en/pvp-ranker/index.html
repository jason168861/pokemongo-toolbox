How PvP IV rankings work

In Great League (1,500 CP cap) and Ultra League (2,500 CP cap) the best IVs are usually not 15/15/15. A lower Attack IV lets a Pokémon reach a higher level under the CP cap, which raises its Defense and HP more than it loses in Attack — so its overall bulk (stat product) ends up higher.

Enter your Pokémon's IVs to see where they rank among all 4,096 combinations in each league, the level and CP to power up to, and how the evolutions in the same family compare. Rank 1 is the best possible spread; outside the most competitive play, anything near the top is usually good enough.

Master League has no CP cap, so a 15/15/15 Pokémon is the best there.

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日 · English version

歡迎使用 Pokémon Go 工具箱（pogokit.com，以下簡稱「本站」）。本頁說明本站如何處理你的資訊。當你使用本站，即表示你同意本政策所述之作法。

一、本站收集的資訊

本站是純前端的靜態工具，不需要註冊、不會要求你提供個人資料。你在 CP 查詢、篩選器等功能中輸入的內容（例如寶可夢名稱），僅在你的瀏覽器本機運算，不會傳送或儲存到本站的伺服器。

交換清單產生器會把清單存在你的瀏覽器。如果你選擇用 Google 登入，清單也會存到 Google Firebase，讓不同裝置同步；分享連結的內容同樣存放在 Firebase，其他人才能開啟。

二、Cookie 與第三方廣告

本站使用第三方廣告服務 Google AdSense 來維持營運：

  • Google 等第三方廠商會使用 Cookie，根據你造訪本站及其他網站的紀錄，放送較符合你興趣的廣告。
  • 你可以前往 Google 廣告設定 停用個人化廣告；或前往 aboutads.info 停用部分第三方廠商的 Cookie。
  • 詳情請參閱 Google 廣告與隱私權政策。
  • 位於歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的訪客，在使用個人化廣告或分析 Cookie 之前，會先透過 Google 的同意訊息徵求你的同意。

三、分析工具

本站使用 Google Analytics 4 收集匿名的彙總使用資料，用以了解哪些功能實際被使用、並據此改善服務。收集的內容包含：

  • 造訪的頁面、裝置類型、大致地區、來源網站
  • 使用了哪一個工具、介面語言
  • 交易清單的操作步驟（例如「開始製圖」「下載圖片」）與所選的圖片樣式
  • 在搜尋框輸入後找不到結果的關鍵字——用來補充寶可夢的別名對照表，讓別人搜同一個詞時找得到
  • 介面操作的反應時間（例如某個按鈕是否反應過慢、是哪一個按鈕），用來找出需要改善效能的地方；不包含你輸入的內容

本站不會將以下內容送出：你的訓練家代碼、卡片備註、清單內容，以及分享連結的識別碼。這些只存在你的瀏覽器（或你自行登入後的雲端清單）中。輸入內容若包含疑似訓練家代碼的數字串，也會在送出前被自動濾除。

你可以安裝 Google Analytics 停用外掛程式，或在瀏覽器開啟「請勿追蹤」／使用內容封鎖器來停止這類收集。

四、第三方連結

本站可能包含指向外部網站的連結。這些網站各有其隱私權政策，本站不對其內容或作法負責，建議你於離開本站時自行留意。

五、兒童隱私

本站不刻意向 13 歲以下兒童收集個人資料。

六、你的權利

若你位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）等地區，你可能享有存取、更正或刪除個人資料的權利。由於本站不主動收集可識別個資，相關要求主要適用於第三方服務（如 Google），請直接透過其設定進行管理。

若你位於美國某些州（例如加州），當地法律可能賦予你選擇不讓個人資訊被「出售」或「分享」用於跨情境行為廣告的權利。你可以透過 Google 廣告設定 關閉個人化廣告；本站目前沒有另外提供美國各州的「不出售／不分享」選項。

七、政策更新

本政策可能不定期修訂。修訂後將於本頁公布最新版本及更新日期。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This is an English translation of the policy above; if the two differ, the Traditional Chinese version applies.

Welcome to Pokémon GO Toolbox (pogokit.com, “the site”). This page explains how the site handles your information. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

The site is a static, front-end-only set of tools. No registration is required and we do not ask for personal information. What you type into tools such as the CP checker or the filters (for example Pokémon names) is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

The trade list generator saves your lists in your browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, your lists are also stored in Google Firebase so they can sync between your devices; share links are stored there as well so other people can open them.

2. Cookies and third-party advertising

The site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service, to cover its running costs:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info.
  • See how Google uses information from sites that use its services for details.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used.

3. Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data so we can see which features are actually used and improve them. This includes:

  • pages visited, device type, approximate region and referring site;
  • which tool was used and the interface language;
  • steps taken in the trade list generator (for example “make image” or “download image”) and the image style chosen;
  • search terms that returned no results, used to extend the Pokémon alias table so others can find the same term;
  • how quickly the interface responded to taps and clicks (for example which button was slow), used to find performance problems. This never includes what you typed.

The site does not send your trainer code, card notes, list contents or share-link IDs. These stay in your browser (or in your own cloud lists if you sign in). Text that looks like a trainer code is filtered out automatically before anything is sent.

You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or use “Do Not Track” and content blockers in your browser to stop this collection.

4. Third-party links

The site may link to external websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

5. Children’s privacy

The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or a similar region, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the site does not actively collect identifiable personal data, such requests mainly concern third-party services such as Google; please manage them through those services’ settings.

If you live in certain US states (such as California), you may have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings; the site does not currently offer a separate US state “do not sell or share” option.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version and its date will always be posted on this page.
